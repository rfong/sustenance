tea eggs
---

Recipe from [The Woks Of Life](https://thewoksoflife.com/chinese-tea-eggs/). Makes 12 tea eggs.

## Marinade ingredients
- a thumb's worth of ginger, sliced
- 3 star anise
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 Tbsp black tea leaves; ideally Yunnan
- 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns
- 3 Tbsp light soy sauce
- 4 tsp dark soy sauce (it has a different flavor)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 Tbsp Shaoxing wine

## Instructions
- Combine 4-5 cups water with the marinade ingredients. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 10 minutes. Let cool completely.
- Bring a new pot of water to a boil for the eggs. Add 12 eggs without cracking them. Boil for 7-8 minutes depending how firm you like the yolks.
- Drain and cool the eggs completely in cold water or an ice bath. I just run cold faucet water over them.
- Gently crack the egg shells all over with a spoon or something, to give the marinade space to enter.
- Soak the eggs in the marinade for at least 24 hours in the refrigerator.

The eggs will last for 3-4 days refrigerated.
