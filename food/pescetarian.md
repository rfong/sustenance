# thai coconut poached fish
adapted from [dishingouthealth](https://dishingouthealth.com/thai-coconut-poached-fish/)

Adjust to your spice preference; I prefer medium/regular spicy by East Asian standards which is very spicy by American standards.

Spices
- 2 Tbsp coconut oil
- 1 white onion, thinly sliced or diced
- 1 shallot, thinly sliced or diced
- 1-2 thumbs worth of fresh ginger, thinly julienned or minced
- 1-3 Serrano peppers and/or Thai chiles
- 4 large garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- lemongrass paste to taste, or a few pieces of smashed lemongrass stalk

Other ingredients
- 1 standard 13.5oz can of full-fat coconut milk or cream (Chaokoh or Thai Kitchen brands are decently thick)
- 1 lime, juice & zest (or I just use bottled lime juice if I don't have a lime)
- 2 handfuls baby spinach or other tender greens
- 1.25lbs white fish fillets, seasoned with kosher salt

Toppings
- Handful of fresh cilantro, chopped
- Chili crisp (such as Lao Gan Ma)

## methodology
- Start short-grain white rice in the rice cooker with a glob of coconut oil and some lemongrass.
- Saute the spices in a 10-12" skillet or cast iron. Consider adding the garlic last to avoid burning it. I like to make sure the ginger gets soft enough to eat easily.
- Pour in the coconut milk/cream and lime juice/zest. Bring to a simmer.
- Add the greens. Arrange the fish fillets flat so they're as covered as possible by the liquid. Cover and simmer on low until fish has just started to turn opaque, about 6-8 minutes.
- Remove lemongrass stalks if you used them.
- Serve over rice with the toppings.
