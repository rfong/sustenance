# scallion flower rolls
from [Hua Juan 花卷 | Red House Spice](https://redhousespice.com/scallion-flower-rolls/)

## ingredients
|          | makes 6 | makes 12 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DOUGH | | |
| AP flour           | 250 g | 500 g |
| active dried yeast | 1 tsp | 2 tsp |
| baking powder      | 3/4 tsp | 1-1/2 tsp |
| sugar              | 1 tsp | 2 tsp |
| lukewarm water     | 130 mL | 260 mL |
| SCALLION PASTE | | |
| scallions, finely chopped | 2 Tbsp | 1/4 cup |
| five-spice powder or ground Sichuan pepper | 1/4 tsp | 1/2 tsp |
| salt | 1/4 tsp | 1/2 tsp |
| oil  | 1 Tbsp | 2 Tbsp |

## methodology (for 6 bao)
- Dough: mix the ingredients and knead until smooth.
- Scallion paste: heat the oil and pour over the other ingredients. Mix well.
- Roll the dough out onto a floured surface into a thin rectangle, 20cm x 30cm or roughly 8" x 12". Evenly spread the scallion paste over the dough.
- Starting from the long side, fold the dough over itself twice to make a 3 layer strip. Cut crosswise into 12 pieces.
- Shaping: Stack up 2 pieces. Press a chopstick into the middle along the long direction. Then stretch it out long and twist it up around a chopstick into a bao shape.
- Rest 30 minutes.
- Heat oil over high heat. Pan fry the rolls, leaving ample space between them. When the bottoms are brown, pour in 200mL water and cover with a lid to steam. Uncover when the water is gone. 
- Store in an airtight container. Steam to revive them.

See the pictures from [the original recipe](https://redhousespice.com/scallion-flower-rolls/) to get a better sense of the shaping method.
